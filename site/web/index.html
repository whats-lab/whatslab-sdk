About this preview
  • These are the hands officially supported by whatslab-sdk 1.0.0: the 43 human-like four- and five-finger hands of dexhand-description 1.0.0. Each one is retargeted with the DexPilot config bundled in the SDK.
  • URDFs and meshes come from each maker's or lab's official repository; every hand lists its source, commit and license. We do not rehost meshes: your browser downloads them from upstream at a pinned commit.
  • The first time you open a hand, the mesh download can take a while (a few MB to tens of MB; the size is shown below). After that the browser cache makes it fast.
  • Hands whose mesh license forbids redistribution show their joints only.
  • Motions are synthetic hand motions retargeted ahead of time. Live camera is approximate webcam tracking.
