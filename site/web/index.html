Dexterous hands you can teleoperate

This site is built with whatslab-sdk. The hands are collected in dexterous-hand-urdf from each maker's official repository. Every hand shown here can be teleoperated with whatslab-sdk: DexPilot retargeting from OpenXR hand tracking or an AirGlove.

Open a hand to watch it follow a human hand through six synthetic motions, retargeted ahead of time with the DexPilot config bundled in the SDK.

About

  • URDFs come from each maker's or lab's official repository and are collected, with any fixes listed in each hand's PATCHES.txt, in dexterous-hand-urdf. Every hand page links its source repository, commit, license and license files.
  • Meshes are not rehosted. Your browser downloads the upstream originals at a pinned commit, so the first visit to a hand can take a while (a few MB to tens of MB, shown on each hand page); after that the browser cache makes it fast.
  • Hands whose mesh license forbids redistribution (Proception ProHand Gen2) show their joint angles only.
  • The human hand is the WebXR generic hand from immersive-web/webxr-input-profiles (MIT, Copyright 2019 Amazon).
  • Retargeting runs in whatslab-sdk 1.0.0, which supports the 43 hands of dexhand-description 1.0.0 with a bundled DexPilot config for each.
